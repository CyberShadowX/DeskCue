# Support

**English** | [中文](#中文)

## English

| I want to… | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Report a bug / suggest a feature / ask a question | [DeskCue Issues](https://github.com/CyberShadowX/DeskCue/issues); the in-app **Feedback** entry opens this public page |
| Read guides | [Usage and updates](deskcue/使用与更新.md) and [README](README.md) |
| Report a security problem | Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); keep details private |
| Download a release | This fork's [Releases](https://github.com/CyberShadowX/DeskCue/releases) |

The in-app entry does not automatically upload a diagnostics package. Review anything you choose to attach: Issues and their attachments are public. Remove personal content, credentials, and unrelated logs. Reply and resolution times are not guaranteed; no commercial support is offered.

DeskCue is derived from [DeskBox](https://github.com/Tianyu199509/DeskBox). The upstream author's contact addresses, feedback services, and website are not support or download channels for this fork. Attribution remains in [UPSTREAM.md](UPSTREAM.md).

## 中文

| 我想要… | 去哪里 |
| --- | --- |
| 反馈问题 / 提建议 / 问用法 | [DeskCue Issues](https://github.com/CyberShadowX/DeskCue/issues)；应用内「反馈」入口打开此公开页面 |

应用内入口不会自动上传诊断包。请自行检查选择上传的内容：Issue 及其附件是公开的，应移除个人内容、凭据和无关日志。不承诺回复或解决时限，不提供商业支持。

DeskCue 派生自 [DeskBox](https://github.com/Tianyu199509/DeskBox)。上游作者的联系方式、反馈服务和网站不是本分支的支持或下载渠道。归属见 [UPSTREAM.md](UPSTREAM.md)。
