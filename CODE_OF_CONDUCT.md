# Code of Conduct

**English** | [中文](#中文)

---

## English

### Our pledge

DeskCue is a community around a free, open-source desktop tool. Everyone should feel welcome to ask questions, report problems, and share ideas — in English, Chinese, or any language you're comfortable with.

### Our standards

Be kind and constructive. Examples of behavior that supports a healthy community:

- Asking and answering questions patiently, including beginner questions.
- Giving reproduction steps, versions, and screenshots in bug reports.
- Disagreeing about features or design on the merits, without personal remarks.
- Respecting that priorities and roadmaps belong to the fork maintainer.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or trolling.
- Spam, advertising, or off-topic promotion.
- Publishing other users' private information.
- Demanding immediate fixes, refunds-style pressure, or hostile escalation.

### Enforcement

Use GitHub's reporting tools for unacceptable behavior. To request a private channel from this fork's maintainer, follow the contact instructions in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md#reporting-a-vulnerability); do not post private details in public Issues. Enforcement is at the maintainer's discretion and may include warning, comment removal, or ban. The upstream author's email is not a DeskCue conduct-report address.

## 中文

### 我们的承诺

DeskCue 是围绕一个免费开源桌面工具的社区。任何人提问、报告问题、分享想法都应受到欢迎——用英文、中文或你熟悉的任何语言。

### 行为标准

保持友善和建设性。有助于社区健康的行为例如：

- 耐心提问与解答，包括新手问题。
- 反馈 Bug 时提供复现步骤、版本号和截图。
- 就功能或设计发表不同意见时对事不对人。
- 尊重本分支的优先级与路线图由分支维护者决定。

不可接受的行为：

- 骚扰、人身攻击、歧视性言论或恶意挑衅。
- 垃圾信息、广告或无关推广。
- 公布他人的隐私信息。
- 强索立即修复、施压或敌意升级。

### 处理

请使用 GitHub 举报功能。需要向本分支维护者请求私密渠道时，遵循 [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md#如何报告漏洞) 的联系说明，不在公开 Issue 中发布私密细节。处理方式由维护者裁量，包括警告、删除评论或封禁。上游作者的邮箱不是 DeskCue 行为举报地址。
